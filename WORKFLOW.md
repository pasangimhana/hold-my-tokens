# Workflow

## 1. Kickoff

OpenAI had just dropped 719 AI-written math papers on GitHub ([openai/math](https://github.com/openai/math)).

> Make a launch video for OpenAI's new math release. It should look scientific and mathematical: math diagrams, LaTeX fonts, line diagrams, complex equations, lots of animation.
>
> Cover the proofs, and that exponential AI progress graph with the "you are here" moment.
>
> Fast-paced, motivational and cool as fuck. Two years ago these models were so-so at math. Now they're incredible.
>
> But no bullshit. Everything has to be based on the repo and real progress.
>
> Use a slowed Macarena.

## 2. Finding the look

v1 was flat 2D.

> This is [not the vibe]. I want a launch-video style with 3D and motion graphics.

v2 was neon 3D motion graphics.

> Good design, but too corny. Make something similar in 3D with a more mathematical vibe: black and white, math fonts.

Instead of one more attempt, I asked for options.

> Now make 5 different variations of this.

Five full films came back: Swiss, Plaster, Paper, Engraving and Blackboard. Paper was black ink on warm paper, with 3D figures drawn like a pen plotter.

> The 3rd one is good. Let's iterate on that.

## 3. Iterating

The first Paper cut was 4:3.

> Make it 16:9.

> It's a bit too fast. The equations aren't readable and some things are cropped.

> Make the Lean tick plain green.

> The "you are here" part is too complex. Show where we are now, then shoot the curve up with a shake on "You are here."

> It needs more soul, and it's clunky in places.
